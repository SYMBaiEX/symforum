# Community conduct

We want people with different backgrounds and levels of experience to be able
to build here. Be respectful, explain disagreements and focus criticism on the
work. Assume good intent while taking concerns seriously.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, deliberate
spam, impersonation and publishing private information are not acceptable.
Do not use the project to target or coordinate abuse of other communities.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation or exclude
participants when needed to protect the community. Apply these expectations to
project discussions, issues, pull requests and official community spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to **austin@symbaiex.com**. Share only the
information needed to understand the concern. Maintainers will limit disclosure
to what is necessary to investigate and respond, without promising absolute
confidentiality or a fixed response time.

Participation does not grant permission to access production services or other
people's data. Security reports belong in the process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
